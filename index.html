Running Hollowcreek Farm

  1. Till grass inside a fenced field with the hoe. Clear rocks, stumps and weeds first.
  2. Plant seeds on tilled soil.
  3. Water at each growth stage. Dry crops grow at a quarter of the speed. Refill at the well or the pond.
  4. Harvest when crops sparkle, then ship them at the crate beside the farmhouse.
  5. Replant by hitting a growing crop twice with the hoe to dig it up. Ripe crops get harvested first.
  6. Reinvest in better seeds, tool upgrades, sprinklers, scarecrows and new fields.

Crows show up from level 3 and peck at growing crops. Tap one to chase it off, or place scarecrows. As you level up and buy land, crows come more often, in bigger flocks, eat faster and hop from crop to crop, so unprotected fields get wiped out. Your farm saves in this browser and keeps growing while you're away (up to 2 hours), unless you pause before you leave.
